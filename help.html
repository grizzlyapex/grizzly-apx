Help Center

Running into an issue loading the site or submitting a form? Try the steps below before reaching out.

Most common issue

The site looks broken or won't load

This is usually caused by an old cached version in your browser. Try: (1) opening the site in Incognito/Private mode, (2) a hard refresh with Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac), or (3) clearing your browser's cache from settings.

Make sure the link is typed in full and correctly. Also check your internet connection, and try opening the site from a different browser or device to see if the issue is general or specific to your device.

Make sure your email and password are entered correctly. If it's your first time, select the 'Create account' tab instead of 'Sign in'. You can also try signing in with Google instead of email.

Make sure the required fields (name and project idea) are filled in. Check your internet connection, and try refreshing the page, then sign in again and fill the form once more.

Reach out to us directly and we'll help you as soon as possible — by email, phone, or Instagram. See the contact details at the bottom of this page.
